Note to myself: the problem might be related to the display cutout (notch/punch hole) which prevents the view from drawing into that area, leaving a black bar or letterbox effect on phones like Poco F5, regardless of `RESIZE_MODE_FILL`. To fix this, we need to add `window.attributes.layoutInDisplayCutoutMode = WindowManager.LayoutParams.LAYOUT_IN_DISPLAY_CUTOUT_MODE_SHORT_EDGES` in `CustomPlayerActivity`'s `onCreate` or `hideSystemUI()`.
